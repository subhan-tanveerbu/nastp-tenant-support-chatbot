NASTP TENANT SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
===================================================
This document contains approved information for the
NASTP Tenant Support Chatbot. Only information written

---------------------------------------------------
1. ABOUT NASTP
---------------------------------------------------
The National Aerospace Science & Technology Park (NASTP)
is a technology park designed to bring together aerospace,
IT, cybersecurity, and emerging technology companies under
one collaborative ecosystem. NASTP provides infrastructure,
office space, and a supportive environment for startups,
established companies, and research organizations to grow
and innovate together.

NASTP is organized into themed "Chapters," each focused on
a specific technology domain, and offers shared facilities,
business support services, and a community of like-minded
tenants and partners.

---------------------------------------------------
2. TENANT ONBOARDING
---------------------------------------------------
To become a NASTP tenant, an organization must complete the
following general steps:

Step 1: Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) through the
        official NASTP EOI portal or by contacting the
        NASTP business development team.

Step 2: Attend an initial consultation/interview with the
        NASTP onboarding team to discuss space requirements,
        chapter alignment, and business objectives.

Step 3: Receive a formal offer letter outlining available
        space, chapter placement, and commercial terms.

Step 4: Sign the tenancy agreement and complete the required
        documentation (company registration, NOCs, and
        compliance paperwork as applicable).

Step 5: Complete onboarding orientation, receive access
        credentials, and move into the allocated space.

The typical onboarding process takes a few weeks depending
on space availability and documentation completeness.

---------------------------------------------------
3. EXPRESSION OF INTEREST (EOI)
---------------------------------------------------
The Expression of Interest (EOI) is the first formal step
for any organization interested in becoming a NASTP tenant.

What the EOI requires:
- Company name and registration details
- Brief company profile and core business activities
- Preferred chapter (e.g., Aerospace, IT, Cybersecurity, AI)
- Estimated team size and space requirements
- Contact person details (name, email, phone)

How to submit an EOI:
- Online: through the official NASTP tenant portal
- Email: submit the EOI form to the NASTP business
  development team at the contact details listed in
  the Contact Information section below

After submission, the NASTP team typically reviews EOIs
and responds within 5-10 business days.

---------------------------------------------------
4. FACILITIES
---------------------------------------------------
NASTP tenants have access to the following facilities:

- Fully furnished office spaces and dedicated desks
- High-speed internet and secure network infrastructure
- Meeting rooms and conference facilities (bookable)
- Shared co-working and collaboration zones
- On-site cafeteria and refreshment areas
- 24/7 security and access-controlled entry
- Parking facilities for tenants and visitors
- Video conferencing and presentation equipment
- Server/data room access (subject to chapter and plan)
- Recreational and break-out areas

---------------------------------------------------
5. CHAPTERS
---------------------------------------------------
NASTP is organized into specialized "Chapters" that group
tenants by technology domain. Current chapters include:

- Aerospace Chapter: companies working in aviation,
  aerospace manufacturing, and related engineering fields.

- Information Technology (IT) Chapter: software houses,
  SaaS companies, and digital product teams.

- Cybersecurity Chapter: companies focused on security
  products, threat intelligence, and consulting.

- Artificial Intelligence & Emerging Tech Chapter: startups
  and teams working on AI, machine learning, robotics, and
  other emerging technologies.

- Research & Academia Chapter: research institutions and
  academic partners collaborating on applied technology
  projects.

Each chapter has its own community events, networking
sessions, and domain-specific support resources.

---------------------------------------------------
6. TENANT SERVICES
---------------------------------------------------
NASTP provides the following support services to tenants:

- IT and network support helpdesk
- Facilities management and maintenance requests
- Business development and partnership facilitation
- Access to industry networking events and workshops
- Assistance with regulatory and compliance matters
- Marketing and visibility support (directory listing,
  featured tenant spotlights)
- Mentorship and advisory sessions (subject to availability)

Tenants can raise service requests through the NASTP tenant
portal or by contacting the support team directly.

---------------------------------------------------
7. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
---------------------------------------------------
Q: How long does the onboarding process take?
A: It typically takes a few weeks from EOI submission to
   move-in, depending on space availability and how quickly
   documentation is completed.

Q: Can I change my chapter after joining?
A: Chapter changes are possible but must be requested through
   the tenant services team and are subject to space
   availability.

Q: Are meeting rooms free to use?
A: Meeting rooms can be booked by tenants; usage policies and
   any applicable limits are shared during onboarding
   orientation.

Q: Is parking available for visitors?
A: Yes, visitor parking is available, subject to space and
   security registration at the entrance.

Q: Who do I contact for IT issues?
A: Tenants should contact the on-site IT/network support
   helpdesk or submit a request through the tenant portal.

Q: Can startups with small teams apply?
A: Yes, NASTP welcomes startups of varying sizes. Space
   options are available for both small teams and larger
   organizations.

9. OUT-OF-SCOPE POLICY
---------------------------------------------------
This chatbot only answers questions related to NASTP tenant
onboarding, EOI, facilities, chapters, tenant services, FAQs,
and contact information, based strictly on the content in
this knowledge base.

The chatbot must NOT answer questions about:
- Topics unrelated to NASTP tenant support
- Personal, medical, legal, or financial advice
- Confidential, internal, or unpublished company information
- Anything not explicitly documented in this file

not available in this document, the chatbot must respond:

"I don't have approved information about that. Please contact
the NASTP support team."
